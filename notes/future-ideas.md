# Future ideas and experiments

These are possible future experiments and questions, not claims of built products. This repository note is not part of the published site's pages or navigation.

## Household Asset & Wardrobe Memory

- **Question:** Can a lightweight household RAG-style system reduce the cognitive load of remembering what a home owns, where it is physically stored, what needs seasonal rotation or maintenance, and what could be reused or shared before something new is purchased?
- **Why it matters:** In Indian homes, storage can be distributed across cupboards, lofts, boxes, rooms and seasonal spaces. Remembering what exists can itself become work.
- **Possible experiment:** Start with one cupboard and a small photo/item inventory mapped to physical storage locations. Try natural-language retrieval, seasonal wardrobe rotation, maintenance reminders, duplicate detection, repair/reuse suggestions, household-level sharing, and digitally matching available items to household needs. Keep the inventory easy to correct when things move.
- **Related current Makex theme:** AI Within Reach; Shared Capacity & Mobility.
- **Status:** EXPLORING. Proposed experiment; not built.

## Household Food & Meal Coordination

- **Question:** Can meal planning combine household preferences and constraints, available ingredients, local recipes and meal rotation without becoming another planning chore?
- **Why it matters:** Households repeatedly coordinate tastes, available food, time and cooking resources. Better coordination could help use ingredients already at home and reduce repetitive planning.
- **Possible experiment:** Try a short meal rotation for one household using available ingredients, member preferences, regional recipes and explicitly provided dietary/health constraints. Let people revise suggestions around preparation time and shared cooking resources. This is meal coordination, not medical diagnosis or treatment.
- **Related current Makex theme:** AI Within Reach; Human + Agent Collaboration.
- **Status:** EXPLORING. Proposed experiment; not built.

## Digital Measurement + Local Tailoring

- **Question:** How could digital measurement/design tools improve custom fit while preserving the role and skills of local tailors?
- **Why it matters:** Local tailoring already provides valuable judgment and craft. Measurement and design support may help communicate fit requirements and reduce repeated adjustments.
- **Possible experiment:** With a willing tailor and participant, compare a simple digital measurement/design record with the tailor's usual process for one garment, documenting corrections and retaining the tailor's final judgment.
- **Related current Makex theme:** Personalized Physical Solutions.
- **Status:** EXPLORING. Proposed experiment; not built.

## Old-Phone Career / Capability Terminal

- **Question:** Can reused phones become low-cost personal or shared capability-building terminals?
- **Why it matters:** A phone that no longer serves well as a primary device may still support learning and career tasks. The useful question is what remains practical on its actual hardware and connectivity.
- **Possible experiment:** On one reused phone, try a small learning and career workflow with saved materials, evidence of skills and resumable tasks. Measure usability, battery/network needs and privacy when a device is shared.
- **Related current Makex theme:** Learning & Capability; AI Within Reach.
- **Status:** EXPLORING. Proposed extension of existing reused-device experiments; this terminal has not been built.

## Indicative Local Business Presence

- **Question:** Can an offline business become minimally discoverable using sparse information before full digitization?
- **Why it matters:** Useful local capability may be absent from searchable catalogues. A minimal presence could help, but incomplete or stale information can also mislead people and burden businesses.
- **Possible experiment:** If revisited, first review the practical concerns from earlier versions with a willing business. Then test a small, clearly indicative listing with a simple correction/removal path before considering wider discovery.
- **Related current Makex theme:** Commerce & Livelihoods.
- **Status:** PAUSED / exploratory. Earlier versions raised practical concerns; no active product claim.
